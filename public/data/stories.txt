2. SMARTATHON
What started as chaos turned into one of the most unforgettable experiences of our journey 💫

We were a team of 6, heading to Smartathon 2.0 with barely any time to prepare. Between our hectic schedules and organizing a hackathon for our own college fest, building a strong prototype felt almost impossible. Still, we packed whatever components we had and headed to Chennai with hope.

The moment we stepped in and saw other teams with massive, polished prototypes… we lost confidence instantly.

But that’s when our mentor, Dr. Monisha ma’am, reminded us: “If your idea is strong, it will stand out.” That stuck with us.

We pushed forward.

Our problem statement—solar mine dewatering—was common among teams, so we knew we had to think differently. After hours of brainstorming, we finally landed on a unique approach… but there was a catch—we didn’t have enough components to actually demonstrate it.

We tried everything. Blinkit. Zepto. No service in that area.

Hope dropped again.

During mid-evaluation, we explained our idea. The jury was impressed—but we were disappointed because we couldn’t show it physically.

Still, we kept going.

22 hours in… final evaluation.

Other teams had prototypes that looked like fully designed modular kitchens. Ours? A mini kitchen set 😂

But something interesting happened.

The judges liked our innovation. They saw potential. Even when our dewatering demo failed at the last minute, they gave us a second chance.

We ran around, borrowed components from nearby teams, fixed things in a rush… and finally—we made it work.

The judge smiled and said, “Good.”

Even then, we weren’t fully satisfied. We’ve given better performances before and walked away with nothing.

So we expected nothing.

Top 10 announcements came—we got in.

Top 2… went to the teams we had been worried about all along (rightly so 😂).

But somewhere, I wondered—what if there’s a special prize?

And right then…

They announced a Special Prize for Innovation.

And it was us.

Out of all the projects in our domain.

We were honestly shocked. We didn’t expect even 1%, but the jury saw something in us—and that meant everything.

None of this would’ve been possible without my amazing team—Madhu Rithika R K, Kripasree Mohanraj, Preethi Vigneswaran, Nandita Shree, and RAJ MOORTHY B. From last-minute problem solving to running around for components and holding onto belief till the very end—we truly did this together 🤝

This journey reminded us:
It’s not always about having the biggest prototype.
It’s about persistence, adaptability, and believing in your idea—even when things fall apart.

Grateful for my incredible team and to Dr.Monisha Ragunathan ma’am for believing in us when we couldn’t.

Sometimes, that’s all you need 💙
